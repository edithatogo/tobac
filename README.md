# tobac

New Zealand tobacco-regulation uncertainty and wastewater value-of-information research.

## Purpose

1. Support decisions about purchasing independent wastewater information.
2. Demonstrate reproducible value-of-information analysis in NZ public health.
3. Make disputed empirical claims testable without presupposing a policy or funding conclusion.

The primary sequence is regulatory-policy EVPI, grouped EVPPI, targeted scientific information gain, then study EVSI and conditional ENBS. Baseline exposure measurements do not automatically identify product legality, causal policy effects, or organised-crime harm.

This repository is being initialised from the verified local decision-programme material. The regulatory milestone, reproducibility instructions, results and provenance will be added as a tested commit. No current NZ posterior, commissioned study or approved regulatory recommendation is claimed by this initialisation.
