# Longitudinal assay, lawful-consumption and price request addendum

Prepared for Dylan A Mordaunt, 24 October 2026. Draft only; NOT SENT. Append to the previously prepared research/laboratory requests, without implying that their recipients have replied.

## Data

Please supply de-identified treatment-plant/catchment IDs, exact date/time and collection duration, paired cotinine/3HC/anabasine/anatabine values, units, per-assay LOD/LOQ and censoring flags. Include all attempted collections and missingness reasons, not only successful quantifications. Provide flow, population method and uncertainty, coverage changes, rainfall where available, sample handling, storage duration, instrument method, laboratory and batch, recoveries/internal standards, replicates and bridge specimens across changes. Keep technical injections distinct from biological/collection-day repeats. Method-validation data for anabasine below the concentration range previously quantifiable are especially relevant.

For lawful supply, please provide monthly or quarterly product-specific releases/sales with definitions, correction history, stock information and geographic coverage where permissible. Explain the mass conversion behind the 2010–2025 official tonne series and whether domestic/duty-free scope matches the reconstructed cigarette-equivalent series. National supply must not automatically be allocated to wastewater catchments by population alone.

For surveys, request the current-vintage complete time series and uncertainty (preferably replicate-weight or variance information), group definitions, fieldwork intervals and revisions; avoid summing overlapping ethnicity groups. Age-specific outcomes require direct supporting data.

## Design and pricing

Quote feasibility/assay validation separately from recurring collection. Compare 12 sites with monthly, paired monthly and fortnightly schedules over a full calendar year; state actual date spacing, season coverage, missed-sample handling and turnaround. Include setup, ongoing QC, optional revalidation, residual-sample access and matrix-specific requirements. Specify incremental versus allocated costs, GST, price validity, volume breaks and capacity. Shared collection is not assumed free. Avoid charging the feasibility pilot twice when included in a first-year programme.

A three-month pilot is a potential feasibility step, not proof that annual seasonality is calibrated. A second year and method-bridge specimens may be needed to separate seasonal recurrence, secular change and altered coverage. Please identify which existing historical specimens and metadata can be reused with appropriate permissions.
