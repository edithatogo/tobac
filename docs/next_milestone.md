# M2 — Make the regulatory screening empirically decision-relevant

## Proposed research contract
Choose one deliverable NZ regulatory contrast, not an undefined blanket ban. Record its eligible products/population, implementation, comparator, timing, accompanying cessation access and enforcement, and what outcome could reverse the decision. Current legal settings must be verified before field commissioning. No agency endorsement is assumed.

## Evidence packages and contingencies

| Package | Required output | Why and contingency |
|---|---|---|
| Time-aligned current state | Matched total consumption, lawful consumption, stock/visitor/duty-free adjustments and uncertainty | WBE plus supply can estimate a discrepancy; without bias qualification it is not a precise illicit total. Keep negative observed gaps as discrepancies. |
| Causal contrast | Prespecified longitudinal/comparison design, counterfactual and confounders | Baseline WBE does not identify regulation-induced diversion. If no credible contrast exists, use explicit elicitation and keep results conditional. |
| Mitigation effectiveness | Net consumption and illicit-volume response to accompanying measures | Seizures are not automatically prevented consumption. Include substitution and displacement. |
| Criminal consequences | Evidence linking incremental market activity to additional harms and resource costs | Participation, turnover, profit, tax loss and harm are distinct. Preserve a zero-marginal-harm case. |
| Assay qualification | Raw-scale anabasine limits, yield distribution, sample recovery, shared errors and reporting delay | Compare better quantification with biological calibration; do not buy more samples to address a shared bias. |
| Programme feasibility | Incremental real costs, agency actions, equity constraints and Maori governance | Budget does not equal consent. Catchment chemistry cannot identify ethnicity or criminal participants. |

## Acceptance criteria
- Existing evidence is conditioned on once and dependencies are recorded.
- Policy effects and criminal harms are not inferred from baseline exposure without an explicit causal pathway.
- Primary priors are source-supported or transparently elicited, with alternative priors/structures reported separately.
- Held-out field predictions and source-specific bias challenges are evaluated, not just numerical unit tests.
- Recompute EVPI and grouped EVPPI before optimising assay/sample design; do not add group values.
- Evaluate scientific discrimination, targeted EIG, same-data prior robustness, EVSI and cost ceilings separately.
- Investigate implementation-adjusted value only with an evidence-to-adoption pathway, not an invented monetary benefit of defeating rhetoric.
- A negative or no-study finding remains publishable. No claim that all normative differences are resolvable by measurement.

The secondary cessation and optional health/fiscal engines remain available in the verified predecessor archive. Reintroduce them only when needed by the chosen consequence model; do not assign their lifetime benefits directly to surveillance.
