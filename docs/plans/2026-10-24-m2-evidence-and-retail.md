## Scope and rulings
- Primary real policy question: prospectively evaluate a specified NZ smoked-tobacco retail licensing cap (600 sites as a historical design precedent), separately from nicotine-content and generational restrictions. The historical scheme was suspended/repealed before its planned implementation: not a realised natural experiment.
- Published Beta(23.4,97.2) is a MODELLED additional net-cessation parameter for about 300 outlets / 95% reduction, not a measured causal effect or M1's gross displacement. Use only a separately labelled transported structural case; retain zero and attenuated effects.
- Add an explicit probability of lawful replacement after illicit-market disruption; do not assume every displaced illicit purchase becomes a quit.
- Observations require source/data identity, matching state identity, date/space/unit metadata, and declared dependence/transport. Forecasts are not observation likelihoods.
- Existing evidence must condition D0 once. Realised incremental study value and prospective joint-minus-first value are different outputs.
- Do not modify the other branch, change privacy, weaken hosted gates, or claim installed optional libraries without execution.
