# Reporting crosswalk for the conditional manuscript

This is an applicability crosswalk, not a claim of full economic-evaluation
compliance or a methodological-quality score. It uses the retrieved primary
[CHEERS-VOI source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jval.2023.06.014), Table 2, printed
pp 1464–1466 (visually checked), and its base CHEERS 2022 item correspondence.

The source prose reports 33 items, while the displayed table has 34 distinct topic
identifiers:1–28 and S1–S6. Every displayed identifier is retained. Subparts S 1 a–c
and S 3 a–b are covered within their parent rows. This records the discrepancy without
inventing a correction or contacting the authors.

The article is a conditional normalised-score illustration. Monetary, QALY,
research-population extrapolation and priced-study requirements cannot be claimed
satisfied by synthetic scores. Unestimated heterogeneity/equity and absent engagement
are reported as such; missing author funding/interests remain required facts.
