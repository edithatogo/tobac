## Applicable guidance and assessment

HDEC scope guidance describes review triggers involving participants,tissue and health
information,including identifiability/linkability. Its scope is not a universal rule
that every public-data paper needs HDEC approval. The HDEC FAQ also states that being
outside HDEC scope does not exclude institutional or journal ethics requirements.
HDEC does not provide retrospective approval. No out-of-scope letter or determination
has been obtained and none is claimed. The current institution,research location/site
and intended journal are not specified,so the applicable formal route remains undetermined.
This is a publication-readiness limitation,not an invented approval or an instruction
to submit a form. If formal review applies,it must be addressed prospectively before
in-scope work;no retrospective cure is assumed.

NEAC's Research and Māori guidance addresses cultural rigour,Māori interests,data
sovereignty and proportionate engagement,including general-population research relevant
to Māori. Public availability does not itself remove these considerations. This desk
assessment cannot certify compliance,decide for Māori stakeholders or manufacture
consultation. The current model estimates no Māori-specific effects and elicits no Māori
preferences. National-average results must not be described as distributionally equitable
or representative of Māori views. No consultation or partnership has occurred through
this work;the appropriate institutional/local determination is unresolved.

For the current bounded analytical package,the supportable claim is that public sources
and explicitly hypothetical value profiles have been used transparently. No ethics
approval,waiver,community endorsement or permission for deployment is stated. Formal
institutional/journal requirements and any applicable cultural review remain author-level
readiness items before submission;they are not solved by software tests or this document.
