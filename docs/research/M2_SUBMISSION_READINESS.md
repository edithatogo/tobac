## Empirical gaps and what they mean for this article

Matched causal policy response, current consumption/supply reconciliation, target
assay/calibration dependence, incremental criminal harms, actual resource/study costs
and distributional effects remain unidentified by the reviewed public evidence.
A decision alone does not supply those observations. They limit empirical effect,
funding and equity claims; they do not require soliciting new data to report this
explicitly conditional analysis. Future suitable public evidence could change the
assessment, but no request or background monitor is authorised or created.
