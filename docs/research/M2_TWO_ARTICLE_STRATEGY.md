## Original remit and what the present analysis establishes

| Proposition | Present support | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tobacco wastewater testing could narrow factual disagreement | Plausible measurement-and-decision proposition; existing marker research supports possible measurement roles | No observed NZ belief convergence, agreement rate or diagnosed ideological groups |
| A decision model can estimate the impact of better information | Existing EVSI computes expected improvement in a stated objective for optimal report-responsive decisions | It does not estimate political persuasion, consensus, delayed adoption or actual policy effects |
| A model can assess whether the measurement would be worth it | Gross EVSI can be compared with compatible study/delay costs; costs and uptake can be varied explicitly | Actual performance, uptake and costs are unidentified; normalised scores are not money |
| Testing resolves the ideological divide | Not established | Different values, trust and implementation remain; agreement is not a welfare metric |

The user's remit is preserved as a conditional proposition with a partial analytical
answer. It must not be replaced by an evidence-map-only article, or represented as an
executed model of ideological convergence. No new actor-belief model is introduced.

| Dimension | MDM research article | NZMJ Viewpoint |

| Question | How do values, priors and report assumptions change conditional choices and information priorities? | How could tobacco wastewater measurement narrow factual disagreement, and how could its decision value be assessed? |

Both manuscripts must remain independently useful. A journal label, shorter text or
new audience alone does not establish a second contribution. Related manuscripts and
any shared material must be disclosed in any later authorised submission, with copies
and cross-references appropriate to status and blinded-review requirements. Do not
claim that an unpublished companion is peer-reviewed or accepted. The current NZMJ
source identifies the companion by title as an unpublished manuscript; update the formal reference when actual bibliographic details exist.
