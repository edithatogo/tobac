# Owner decisions to progress issue #3

These options follow the existing-record assessment. Recommendations are not adopted
choices, access permissions, scientific priors or authority to contact custodians.
The analytical reference remains NZ residents aged 15+ using cigarettes/roll-your-own;
catchment-specific inference additionally needs matched geography and denominators.

| Decision | Recommended next choice | Alternative and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research endpoint | Keep the 600-retailer contrast as explicitly hypothetical; progress conditional identification and record feasibility. No current causal policy claim. | Select an actually deliverable intervention and comparator, with dates and geography, to pursue empirical causal qualification. This changes the study target and must be specified before matching outcomes. |
| Existing-record access | Choose which custodian requests to pursue first: the 2023 wastewater sample/QC/flow/population records and paired human-yield records; then relevant survey vintages and local lawful supply/stock records. Authorise any eventual external request separately with named recipient/content. | Public/retained evidence only. Partial-identification results can continue, but inaccessible raw records remain an explicit limit. |
| Claim if records are inadequate | Retain wide bounds/no-study as an acceptable result. Assess existing records before commissioning collection. | Pursue additional assay/product validation, field outcomes or named-expert elicitation. The owner must select which claim matters; elicitation supplies conditional judgments, not empirical causal validation. |
| Consequence perspective | Assess societal health/resource consequences, with fiscal receipts/transfers and incremental crime harm shown separately. Keep normative weights as sensitivity cases until chosen. | Select a regulator-budget or other explicit perspective and the outcomes it includes. For any decision recommendation, choose acceptable health/crime/equity trade-offs; the assistant cannot infer them from fees or seizures. |
| Spending and commitment | Keep this stage to analysis/access feasibility with no paid commitment. Prepare cost requests once scope is fixed. | Set a maximum spend, funder and permitted purchase scope for quotations/validation/collection. No numerical budget is inferred from historical fees or appropriations. |
| Governance and permitted output | Identify the legitimate Māori governance/data-custodian pathway, permitted linkage/geographic detail and intended research output before restricted-data use or operational deployment. | Retain public aggregate conditional analysis while those decisions are unresolved. Naming a pathway is not a substitute for custodian or Māori governance decisions. |

The first decision controls the others. A conditional research endpoint does not require
waiting for implementation of the historical cap. An empirical endpoint needs a real
intervention/counterfactual; baseline records and a survey of hypothetical responses
cannot create that contrast. No new sample count, provider or field campaign is
selected in this decision register.
