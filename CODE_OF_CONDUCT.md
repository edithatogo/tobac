# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in this project's issues, pull requests and
discussions. Discuss evidence, methods and code on their merits. Give
constructive feedback, take corrections in good faith and respect others'
privacy.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual
attention and sharing someone's private information without permission are
unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive behaviour. Keep personal details out
of public issues and pull requests. [Report abuse on GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

The repository maintainer may remove content, lock conversations or restrict
participation to protect contributors.
