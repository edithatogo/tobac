Dear survey data team,

I am assessing whether existing New Zealand Health Survey (NZHS) records can support
conditional research on tobacco consumption before considering new collection.

2022/23–2024/25 and the periods overlapping the May–October 2023 wastewater campaign?

other product coverage, vaping/dual use, quit attempts/history, and available survey

wording/denominators and skip logic, missingness and any collection interruptions.

Integrated Data Infrastructure (IDI), or require another approved route? The dated access

period and geography could be available or aggregated for matched analysis, what
small-cell limits apply, and whether any linkage is permitted. Repeated cross-sections
would not be treated as an individual longitudinal panel. Respondents aged 15+ and
all-age wastewater denominators are different populations requiring explicit treatment.

Please identify the custodian, permitted purposes, eligibility/application and Māori governance
considerations, public or custodian-produced aggregate alternatives and
existing access charges. No respondent extract or linkage is requested now; no
application, ethics approval, funding or paid work is represented by this enquiry.
