Dear Lisa Pilkington,

I am preparing a conditional research assessment of what existing measurements can
identify about tobacco consumption. I am writing about your study, “Understanding
nicotine consumption habits in Aotearoa New Zealand using wastewater-based epidemiology”
(DOI10.1016/j.scitotenv.2025.181223). The immediate purpose is to assess existing
record coverage before considering new measurements, not to request a new analysis
or sample collection. This is not an evaluation of an implemented 600-retailer cap.

Could you advise who holds the relevant data and whether a dictionary or non-sensitive
coverage summary is available for the following existing records?

- Sample/date/catchment and analytical batch pairing for the 2023 campaign, including
  missing specimens, repeats, exclusions, and reasons; coarsened/pseudonymous coverage
  is sufficient at this stage, with no catchment key or identifying location requested.
- Native concentrations and units for anabasine, anatabine, cotinine and
  trans-3'-hydroxycotinine; distinction between measured and derived values;
  below-detection/below-quantification flags and sample/batch thresholds.
- Original-influent versus extract basis, extraction/reconstitution volumes,
  dilution/enrichment, recovery treatment, calibration/internal standards, free versus
  hydrolysed forms, blanks, spikes, precision and storage/stability records.
- Contemporaneous flow and contributing-population definitions, uncertainty and
  missingness, including how visitors/commuters and all-age coverage were handled.
- Availability of paired quality-control (QC) or sufficient summaries to distinguish random assay error
  from shared batch, flow, population and correction-factor uncertainty; derivation
  code and source dependencies for the published loads, if already retained.

The R1 rows of the field schedule describe possible later analysis fields. For now,
please indicate whether each is held, its period/resolution, relevant limitations,
and which access/governance pathway would apply. Please do not send row-level or
restricted records by reply. Public metadata links or existing custodian-produced aggregate
summaries would be useful alternatives. Information about existing access/preparation
charges is welcome, but this enquiry does not authorise paid work or a quotation for
new experiments. Any later transfer would need an agreed permitted purpose and route.

Thank you for considering this feasibility enquiry.
