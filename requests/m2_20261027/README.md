access conditions. The attached-style field schedule is for feasibility review;
it is not a request to email raw records. Row-level transfer, linkage, storage and
publication need a later exact scope and permissible route. Aggregate/custodian-run
summaries are fallbacks; no anonymous or de-identified label alone proves safe use.

Draft bodies exclude internal signoff text and must be reviewed with the named
recipient and owner-approved sender identity before any later sending. Do not infer
institutional affiliation, ethics approval, funding, researcher eligibility, custodian
permission or Māori governance endorsement from these repository files. No CC list
or deadline is imposed, and no offer to pay is made.

R1/R2 first-wave feasibility priorities: native concentration and censoring metadata,
paired calibration/QC and shared errors. R3/R4 are ready as conditional metadata drafts;
they do not assert availability of an exact NZHS vintage or local company volumes.

initial responses need only indicate availability, coverage and limitations.

must retain underlying-data identities so published loads, returns aggregates and
