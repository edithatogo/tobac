Subject: Tobacco return definitions and coverage for matched consumption research

Dear tobacco returns data team,

I am assessing existing records for conditional tobacco research. Could you advise on
available dictionaries, reporting definitions and permissible aggregate summaries for
2023–2025 cigarette and roll-your-own returns, particularly any period overlapping the
2023 wastewater campaign? This is a feasibility enquiry, not a request for a new survey
or disclosure of confidential company/local records by email.

The R4 schedule concerns native-unit product volumes and revisions; domestic versus
duty-free or other channels; releases versus sales; stock/returns, visitors/personal
imports and lawful unrecorded supply; and the availability/limitations of monthly or
local allocation. Please identify double-counting risks across manufacturers,
distributors, aggregates and channels. Do not treat unspecified adjustments as zero.
Any population consumption mapping would require separate assumptions and evidence.

If corrected public metadata already exists, could you point to clarification of the
2025-linked British American Tobacco (BAT) roll-your-own (RYO) document's
2024 heading and the R&S King pack/carton interpretation?
If no correction exists, please describe what the original units/year refer to.
We can retain alternatives where conclusions are robust; a point total is not assumed
necessary. Please describe any physical mass/conversion definitions rather than assume
a common grams-per-stick factor across products or years.

For now, availability and definitions are sufficient. Please identify the appropriate
custodian, sharing/publication conditions, public aggregate alternatives and existing
preparation charges. No restricted data transfer, paid work or commissioning is
requested or authorised. Any later extract would require a separately agreed scope
and permitted route.

Thank you for considering this metadata enquiry.
