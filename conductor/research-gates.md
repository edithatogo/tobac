# Open research gates

These requirements remain scientifically open after completed software tracks are archived.
A missing estimate, null effect or unidentified quantity is a permissible research result.
No software test supplies missing observations, normative choices or permissible-use authority.

| Gate | Missing evidence or decision | Existing boundary and next supported action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision target | Prospective intervention/comparator, baseline retailer/catchment, population and dates | Specify the actual contrast before transporting national/historical evidence; the historical cap was not implemented. |
| Product-source reconciliation | BAT RYO document year; R&S King pack/carton conflict; missing channels and product/additive coverage | Retain conflicts/nulls in integration/reconciliation_2025/generated/report.json. Qualify a revised source or explicit clarification before aggregation. |
| Physical conversions | Factory-made tobacco mass and a justified 2025 RYO grams-per-stick mapping | Counts and tonnes stay separate. The 755-tonne workbook comparator does not identify a cigarette-equivalent total. |
| Shared observations | Overlapping 2021/2022 windows, period covariance, source identity and error structure | Preserve MOH/NZHS underlying-data IDs; qualify the joint measurement model and condition original observations once. Forecasts/posteriors are not new observations. |
| Source-to-target transport | Current target geography, lawful/unrecorded/stock adjustments and transport assumptions | Use existing Scope/EvidencePacket/SupplyBasis; no automatic longitudinal-posterior import. |
| Policy and biomarker calibration | Net cessation, switching, mitigation/replacement, incremental criminal harm, near-limit assay accuracy and serial dependence | Retain null/structural scenarios; obtain matched observations or explicit documented elicitation before empirical claims. |
| Governance and costs | Māori governance/permissible use, equitable coverage and actual incremental costs | These are substantive decisions/data requirements; no new approval workflow, external contact or fabricated values are introduced. |
| Research-method applicability | Generic EVSI/structural API qualification, continuous support and held-out field prediction | Installed EVPI/finite-partition parity and staged-decision fixtures do not close these broader method/empirical gates. |
| Native archival recovery | Original capture timestamps absent; remote raw-object recovery not demonstrated; companion archive work remains separately owned | Retain null timestamps and edition/extraction identities. Local CAS/WARC fixity is not remote recovery. |
