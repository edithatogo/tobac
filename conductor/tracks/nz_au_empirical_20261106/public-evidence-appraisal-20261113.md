## Wang: national wastewater measurement

[Original report](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.57319).
Locators: Methods/Data Analysis, equations 1–6; Discussion/Limitations;
existing Supplement 1 site-33 quarantine in phase1-wang-appraisal.md.
Anabasine informs tobacco-derived nicotine, not tobacco's legal origin. Origin
accounting subtracts industry-funded legal-sales estimates, creating shared-input
dependence. Excretion variability and incomplete anabasine coverage remain;
non-tobacco nicotine cannot be split reliably between vaping and NRT. Those are
measurement and transport limitations, not proof that every aggregate is invalid.
Retain country/product/time/calibration gaps and the previously verified site-state
conflict. The fresh XML has the same hash as the previously appraised original.
Use: methods/conditional calibration comparison only. No independent outcome,
current NZ likelihood, causal retailer effect or monetary VOI is supplied.

## Hatsukami: short-term nicotine reduction

[PMC author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6372240/).
Locators: Methods/Randomization, Outcomes and Statistical Analysis;
Results/Primary End Points, Table 2; Discussion/Limitations.
The three-arm, 20-week US trial supports biomarker contrasts under its allocation,
missing-data and adherence assumptions. CO is an absolute difference; urinary
3-HPMA and PheT effects are ratios of geometric means. Five existing catalogue
entries reconcile to the intervention specifications and immediate-versus-control
contrasts. The gradual-versus-control null findings are retained in the report;
they are not excluded by selecting the existing catalogue contrast. Free study
cigarettes, nonstudy use, differential dropout and uncertain clinical translation
limit policy transport. No annual cessation probability, retailer-cap effect or
proportional disease-risk reduction is inferred. Shared participants and control
arm prevent treating biomarkers or contrasts as independent trials.

## Banks: mortality

[Publisher full text](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-015-0281-z).
Locators: Methods/Statistical methods; Results/Smoking and all-cause mortality;
Discussion. The overall 2.96 estimate and 2.69–3.25 interval agree with the
candidate catalogue. It is an adjusted all-cause hazard ratio (called RR by the
paper) in older NSW participants after specified disease exclusions. Exposure
misclassification, baseline selection and residual confounding constrain transport.
The main paper checks proportional hazards and alternate exposure/exclusion
specifications. Never-smoker baseline hazards and compatible exposure history
remain necessary; multiplying an already mixed population hazard by this ratio
would misapply it. Retain NSW 45 and Up dependence with Weber. An initially
selected PMC address returned another DOI and was rejected; publisher DOI/title
identity was verified instead. The catalogue's PMID is not independently certified
by this pass and must not be used as the sole identity key.

## Endo: quality of life

[Publisher full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11136-026-04173-4).
Locators: Methods/Data, Health utility score and disutility days, Propensity score
analysis; Table 2/Overall; Discussion/limitations. Eight existing utility means
and intervals match Table 2. They describe overlapping Queensland disease groups,
using the Australian EQ-5D-5L value set; intervals use percentile bootstrap with
15,000 replicates. Table 3 adjusted ATT estimates are different estimands and are
not substituted for Table 2 means. Institutional exclusion, survivorship,
unmeasured disease severity and incomplete temporal confounding limit transport.
Do not sum marginal deficits or interpret them as causal cessation gains. Preserve
QPHS cohort dependence with the ED-cost study. No scoring algorithm is reproduced.
