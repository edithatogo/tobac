## Selection and stopping rule

Select four named reports before further result extraction: Wang national WBE
(10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.57319), Hatsukami nicotine reduction
(10.1001/jama.2018.11473), Banks mortality (10.1186/s12916-015-0281-z),
and Endo utility (10.1007/s11136-026-04173-4). They cover measurement,
short-term intervention response, mortality and quality of life. Existing candidate
values and prior appraisals have already been seen; selection is based on mechanism
coverage and unfinished full-text appraisal, not significance. No subgroup display
is selected: only already catalogued overall estimates are reconciled. Subgroup
claims, causal transport and a fitted policy comparison are outside this slice.

Use existing public originals first, then a bounded official full-text route.
Retain failed retrievals; an inaccessible report remains unreviewed and is not
replaced after inspecting results. Stop after these four source dispositions,
result-specific methods/limitations checks and existing-catalogue reconciliation.
No claim of review of all 2,183 full-text candidates or the residual corpus.
No requests, subscriptions, raw-data applications or new collection.

## Implementation and acceptance

Reuse existing catalogue IDs, alias crosswalk and source-use records. Report all
unmatched identities and scope differences, including uncertainty type, rather
than pooling overlapping catalogues. Original bytes remain outside Git; record
hashes and exact locators only. A verified download is not canonical archival
admission, rights clearance for redistribution, or an observation likelihood.

Evaluate explicit finite actions and state-contingent payoffs with exact rational
arithmetic. Report pairwise advantage bounds and attaining states, common optimal
actions, worst-case regret and counterexamples. These bounds apply only to the
explicit finite set (or its declared convex mixtures), not continuous nonlinear
parameter spaces. Missing payoffs yield no decision result; empty compatible sets
are contradictions, not evidence that all actions dominate. Priors and interval
widths do not establish robust action identification.

Tests must cover ties, conflicting states, dominance, dominated actions, duplicate
IDs, nonfinite/unknown inputs, empty support, stale source hashes and catalogue
unit/source/uncertainty mismatches. Reuse the existing shared evidence admission
contract; introduce no alternative observation-admission route. Self-review is
software/methods work by the assistant, not named human scientific acceptance.
No paper claim, runtime default, empirical observation or native signed pin changes.
