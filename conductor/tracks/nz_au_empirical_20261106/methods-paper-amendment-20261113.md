# Conditional decision-sufficiency amendment — 13 November 2026

Question: after reconciling the two existing candidate catalogues, which explicit
restrictions would make a simple health-accounting decision invariant to the
remaining uncertainty? This implements the authorised public-only methods result.
It does not extend the empirical causal model or claim an estimated policy effect.
Earlier values, the four-report appraisal and the two-state synthetic result have
already been seen. This amendment precedes execution of the new declared scenarios.

## Fixed scope and scenario choices

Use the existing Queensland Table 2 means, no-listed-disease utility 0.936 and
COPD utility 0.780, solely as an illustrative accounting contrast d=0.156 per
avoided disease-person-year. These are descriptive, overlapping-cohort summaries;
they are not an estimated causal utility gain from a tobacco intervention. Hold
population/horizon at one hypothetical target person over one year. Compare
act versus wait using incremental value e*d-c in health-equivalent units.
The avoidance fraction e and burden c are explicit hypothetical restrictions,
not effect estimates, current costs, QALY valuations or probability distributions.
No conversion of money to health or assumptions about societal preferences.

Prespecified scenarios: public inputs alone (e and c unknown, no calculation);
wide box e=[0,1], c=[0,0.1]; minimum-response box e=[0.75,1], c=[0,0.1];
high-burden box e=[0,1], c=[0.2,0.3]; no-burden box e=[0,1], c=0;
exact threshold e=0.5, c=0.078. Repeat the minimum-response box with descriptive
marginal interval rectangle d=[0.933-0.800,0.938-0.759]. This last rectangle
ignores unknown joint covariance and is a declared sensitivity set, not a 95%
joint confidence region. Do not choose restrictions by resulting significance.

The payoff is multi-affine in e,d,c. Its advantage extrema on a closed rectangular
box occur at corners. Enumerate those corners with the existing rational oracle,
retaining co-optimal ties and attaining witnesses. This provides exact bounds for
this accounting function, not for arbitrary dynamic/nonlinear models. Report the
threshold c=e*d and the minimum-response restriction needed for weak dominance.
Keep minimax regret separate from empirical recommendation.

## Reconciliation and stopping rule

Give every remaining 45/41 entry a source/report/estimand disposition, related
identities and rationale. Do not infer independence from unmatched IDs, pool
mortality with incidence, combine trial arms and contrasts as independent data,
or equate new fiscal conventions with measured effects. Preserve edition conflicts,
unknown uncertainty and transport limits. No new likelihood or runtime adoption.
Stop after exhaustive catalogue accounting, seven declared scenario results,
negative controls, and a draft paper/supplement/workbook with a claim register.
Broader systematic review, new fitting, BF2, empirical calibration and native
renewal remain outside this amendment. Scientific and author acceptance are pending.
