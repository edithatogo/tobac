# BF2 execution clarification — 13 November 2026

The frozen benchmark specification remains unchanged. Before the full outcome
table is generated, unused adaptive study slots in the equal-spend sensitivity
are filled with a wastewater sample. Forced reports enter a separate research
belief; they do not alter the original adaptive decisions or its later study
choices. Report the extra samples, their cost and the resulting separate research
Brier score. This preserves the comparison's original acquisition schedule while
making spending equal; it is not a claim that the added information was used.

The static interface witness and focused tests were already executed before this
clarification. Tests exercised matched, stable and extreme regimes, including the
analytic extreme negative control; this is not a prospectively blinded study.
The full withheld-regime tables and their ablations had not been generated or
inspected when the forced-channel rule was fixed. No empirical input was used.
