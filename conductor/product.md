# tobac research purpose

Support defensible NZ tobacco-regulation and research-procurement decisions using evidence,
explicit uncertainty and reproducible computations. Sequence: regulatory EVPI, grouped EVPPI,
targeted scientific learning, study EVSI, then conditional ENBS. No-study and null-effect
outcomes remain permissible. Existing historical longitudinal evidence is not a current
illicit-origin posterior or proof of policy effects. Separate facts, assumptions, normative
choices and unqualified extrapolations. Do not modify this purpose to favour a policy result.

## Requested NZ/AU expansion — 6 November 2026

The [NZ/AU milestone](tracks/nz_au_empirical_20261106/spec.md) extends the target to a
comprehensive NZ policy model, actual Australian wastewater comparator, multiple
perspectives, health-related quality of life, distributional equity, diffusion/competition
and incremental research value. These are requirements, not claims of implemented capability.
